# Overview

Empyre is a text-based role-playing game that combines strategy, skill development, and player interaction. Players build characters, engage in quests, manage resources, and compete with other players in a dynamic, evolving game world. Whether you're new to the game or looking to deepen your understanding of game mechanics, this handbook provides comprehensive guidance to help you get started and master Empyre's features.
